About Measured

Written by an advisor.
Not a content team.

Most mortgage content online is written to rank on Google, not to actually help you. It's vague, generic, and carefully avoids saying anything that might cost a lender a customer.

Measured is different. Every issue is written by Anthony — a working mortgage advisor with access to wholesale lending channels — and covers the specific decisions, rates, and market conditions that actually affect what you pay.

No fluff. No pitch. Just the information that most borrowers only find out about after they've already closed with someone else.

Live rate data, every week
Freddie Mac, MBA, Scotsman Guide, and HousingWire — not last month's numbers.
Wholesale channel access
Rates 0.375–0.50% below what retail borrowers typically see — on the same loan.
Plain English, always
No jargon. No fluff. If it doesn't help you make a better decision, it doesn't go in.
One focused topic per week
Rates, credit scores, housing market, policy changes — one thing at a time, done well.
